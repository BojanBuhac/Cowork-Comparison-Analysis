04External signals

Public vendor roadmap and regulatory research, retrieved 7 September 2026. These are outside the organisation's control but set the dates the programme has to plan around.

Microsoft Agent 365 is generally available

Announced generally available on 1 May 2026 as a centralised control plane to discover, manage, govern and secure AI agents across the environment, together with previews for discovering and managing shadow AI agents.

Why it matters: AI Agent Central v3 already advertises Agent 365 integration. Shadow-agent discovery is the capability that would close the ZAG-6 agent registration and verification gap.

microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/01/microsoft-agent-365-now-generally-available-expands-capabilities-and-integrations/

Copilot Studio agent governance updates

The April 2026 Copilot Studio release added agent governance improvements, intelligent workflows, more control over agent operations and an expanded agent usage estimator; Microsoft has separately published six core capabilities for scaling agent adoption in 2026, covering governance, security and operational readiness.

Why it matters: The usage estimator and operational controls map directly onto the financial-governance pillar — token caps, cost attribution and monitoring — in the internal RACI plan.

microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/blog/copilot-studio/6-core-capabilities-to-scale-agent-adoption-in-2026/

EU AI Act — high-risk obligations enforceable

Obligations for high-risk AI systems took legal effect on 2 August 2026, covering Articles 9–17 for providers and Article 26 for deployers — risk management, data governance, technical documentation and transparency. Reported penalties reach €35M or 7% of global revenue, and one industry analysis put the share of organisations unprepared at 78%.

Why it matters: Paris-based operations put this squarely in scope. The internal governance pack covers data residency and DLP, but no risk classification of the S2P, Sales or ESS agents against Annex III was found.

labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-high-risk-compliance-deadline-20/

Dynamics 365 2026 Release Wave 2

Microsoft ships Dynamics 365 updates on a twice-yearly cadence: wave 1 covers April to September, wave 2 covers October to March. Features reach designated sandbox environments for early access roughly 4–6 weeks before general availability.

Why it matters: Gives a concrete validation window for the D365 integration ahead of the October wave — worth aligning with the Zava Trading onboarding workshop rather than running it cold.

learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/get-started/release-schedule

Agent adoption benchmarks

Gartner forecasts that 40% of enterprise applications will embed task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from under 5% in 2025. Against that, published surveys put roughly 31% of enterprises as actually running agents in production.

Why it matters: The internal S2P target of 80% of business units is well above the current market production rate — an ambitious goal that needs the adoption curve stated explicitly when the roadmap goes to leadership.

digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-adoption-2026-enterprise-data-points

Microsoft Foundry Agent Service

Microsoft positions Foundry Agent Service as the consolidated, OpenAI-compatible successor to overlapping preview surfaces, and continues to publish monthly product updates through August 2026. Classic agents are explicitly unaffected by the Assistants API retirement.

Why it matters: The retirement date passed on 26 August 2026. Confirming which subscription workloads were on the Assistants API — and whether they were purely model-inferencing, in which case the Responses API is sufficient — is the fastest way to close R2.

learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/whats-new-foundry
