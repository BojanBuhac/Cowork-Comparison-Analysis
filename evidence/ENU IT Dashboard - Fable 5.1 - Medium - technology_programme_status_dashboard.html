Agent 365 is positioned as the control plane to observe, govern and secure agents. Priced at USD 15 per user/month standalone or included in Microsoft 365 E7. Previews: shadow-agent discovery via Defender/Intune (June 2026), registry sync with AWS Bedrock and Google Cloud, Windows 365 for Agents.
