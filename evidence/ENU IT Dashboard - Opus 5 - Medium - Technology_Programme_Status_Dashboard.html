ERP pricing benchmarks corroborate the budget gap

Independent 2026 pricing research reports that subscription fees account for only about 40–60% of three-year cloud ERP spend, with the balance in implementation, integrations, sandboxes, storage, training and renewal inflation. That structure matches the internal €390k breakdown (implementation €156k, Year 1 licence €136.5k, three-year support €97.5k) and supports the finding that a €250k target was set below market.

Agent adoption benchmarks put the 80% target in context

Published 2026 enterprise surveys report production AI-agent deployment in roughly the 31%–54% range depending on the study, against far higher rates of experimentation. An 80% enterprise-wide adoption goal for the S2P agent therefore sits well above the reported market average — reinforcing the need for the overdue ZAG-5 compliance criteria and a working agent-registration process before scaling.

Caveat on external figures

The adoption and pricing figures above come from commercial market-research and vendor-adjacent publications, not audited data, and the two adoption studies disagree with each other. Treat them as directional context for the steering committee rather than as validated benchmarks.
