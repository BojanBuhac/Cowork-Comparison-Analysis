Section 7

External Signals

Signal 1 ERP cost overruns are the norm, not the exception

The gap between initial vendor quotes and final project costs routinely lands between 50% and 200%, and budget overruns derail roughly 55% of ERP projects. Software subscription typically represents only 20–30% of total project cost, and hidden costs such as data cleansing, change management and post-go-live stabilisation add a further 15–25%.

What it means for usThe ERPMax bid at +56% sits inside the normal quote-to-actual gap. The €250,000 baseline looks more like an under-scoped estimate than the bids looking expensive.

Source:
  • ERP Implementation Cost Guide: What to Expect in 2026

Signal 2 Our 24-month plan is conservative against market benchmarks

A realistic ERP implementation timeline runs 3–9 months for SMEs, 6–12 months for mid-market companies and 12+ months for large enterprises; staged rollouts by function or site typically run 9–18 months.

What it means for usThe two-wave plan — Finance and HR at Month 18, CRM at Month 24 — carries genuine schedule headroom, which supports the phased element of Option D rather than threatening it.

Sources:
  • ERP Implementation: Phases, Timeline & Cost (2026)
  • ERP Costs in 2026: Pricing Models, Benchmarks, and TCO Guide

Signal 3 Generative AI is now a standard ERP differentiator, and a new governance burden

As of mid-2026 all three major ERP platforms surface generative AI for finance close, invoice extraction, forecasting and conversational support — reducing manual variance but adding new governance considerations. Mandatory e-invoicing, digital tax controls and ISSB/ESG reporting have become operational priorities that the ERP must feed.

What it means for usThe evaluation credited ERPMax for AI-driven automation, but the Statement of Work contains no AI governance or explainability clause. Worth adding before contract finalisation in Weeks 6–7.

Source:
  • SAP S/4HANA Cloud vs Oracle Fusion vs Dynamics 365 Finance — June 2026

Signal 4 Agentic ERP is the 2026 vendor battleground

SAP, Oracle and Microsoft are racing to embed autonomous AI agents into the core ERP layer. At SAP Connect 2025 SAP unveiled 14 new Joule Agents spanning finance, HR, procurement, supply chain and industry-specific scenarios; its Cash Management Agent reduces manual cash positioning time by up to 70% according to SAP research using customer data.

What it means for usCapability that did not exist when the RFP was written is now shipping. A roadmap and release-adoption clause with the selected vendor protects against buying a platform that falls behind.

Source:
  • The $35B ERP Agent War: SAP Joule vs Oracle Fusion vs Microsoft Dynamics in 2026

Signal 5 Data residency is not data sovereignty

A US company operating a data centre in Frankfurt still falls under US jurisdiction through the CLOUD Act and FISA. True GDPR compliance requires both that data is physically located in the EU and that it is processed by an entity subject to EU law rather than the laws of a third country.

What it means for usERPMax's Frankfurt, Amsterdam and Dublin data centres satisfy the residency wording in the Statement of Work but do not by themselves establish sovereignty. This belongs in the Weeks 3–5 security and compliance due diligence, not after signature.

Sources:
  • GDPR Data Residency: The EU Cloud Hosting Rules in 2026
  • Sovereign Cloud vs. Public Cloud: The CTO's Guide to EU Data Residency in 2026
